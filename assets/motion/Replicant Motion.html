Replicant Motion

Eighteen replicants and eighty-plus animations. Every replicant is drawn on the same 8×7 anatomy, and every animation is written against that anatomy, never against a character. Any new replicant drawn on the grid gets all of them for free. The desktop below runs a simple life loop that picks what each replicant does from its energy, mood and social drive.

Desktop

They boot up when the page opens, some on top of the three windows. Drag a replicant and let go to drop it, or fling it to throw it. Short drops get a squash. Longer drops flail or tumble, and the landing is picked at random: splat, pancake, head stuck in the floor, roll, legs in the air, bounce, butt thud, or a rare hero landing. A hard throw into the screen edge usually bounces off; once in a while it splats against the wall and slides down. Tap one and it reacts.

ClassicIdle

Low energy: sits, snacks, recharges, then falls asleep in one of five ways. High mood and energy: runs, dances, does jumping jacks. Low mood: sulks, cries or fumes. High social: waves, claps and follows your cursor.

Anatomy

The contract every replicant and every animation shares. Animations only move these parts; each replicant's own colors ride along.

    Animation library

    Each animation is defined once and plays on seven replicants at a time, started at random points so they never move in lockstep. Shuffle the cast to see the same animations on other replicants.
